# Label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.
#
# Copied from the README so the baseline is judged on exactly the definitions
# written there. baseline.py reads this file.
analysis: An argument carried by a specific, checkable fact (a stat, record, date, figure or quote).
hot_take: A confident opinion, claim, prediction or rant with no support, or only vague support ("almost all", "everyone").
reaction: An immediate emotional, humorous or fan response, with no claim or argument.
news_info: Reports a fact, announcement or piece of logistics (headlines, match threads, squad news), or asks a neutral question, with no opinion stated.
